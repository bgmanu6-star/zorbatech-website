Dr Build Care Stock Register

A mobile stock register for a construction chemicals and hardware shop, with separate owner and staff logins and alerts when stock runs low.

ClientDr Build Care, construction chemicals and hardware
What we builtStock register app for phone and PC
LoginsTwo owners and staff
Where the data livesThe shop's own cloud account

The problem

The shop sells waterproofing products, sealants, tile adhesives, admixtures and other construction chemicals. Hundreds of items come in different sizes and brands, and stock was tracked by memory and handwritten notes. When an item ran out, it was usually noticed only when a customer asked for it.

What we built

  • Inward and outward entries in a few taps, for goods received and goods sold.
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts, with a ready reorder list.
  • Not-moving stock report, so money stuck in slow items is easy to see.
  • Daily sales and stock value on the home screen.
  • Owner and staff logins. Staff can add entries. Only owners can edit, delete and manage logins.
  • One shared record on every phone. Entries made on one device show up on the others.
  • Works on a phone like an app, and on a PC at the counter. Excel export and backup are built in.
  • The shop's own data account. The records are stored in an account owned by the shop, and the shop can control who has access.

Screens above use sample products and figures, not the shop's real records.

Delivered

The app was handed over in November 2026, with owner and staff logins set up and a one-page usage guide.
