FAQ

Questions, answered

How much will it cost?
It depends on what you need. After a short conversation you receive a written quote with a fixed scope and price. We favour one-time pricing, with an optional support plan afterwards.
Do I have to pay monthly fees?
No monthly fee is required to use what we build. If a project needs a paid service such as a domain name, it is listed clearly in your quote. Ongoing support is optional and priced separately.
Will I own my data and accounts?
Yes. Your accounts and data are set up in your name, and we hand over every login in writing when the project is finished.
Will the app work on both Android and iPhone?
Yes. We build installable web apps that run on Android, iPhone and computers from a single codebase, without app-store fees or review delays.
Can you improve my existing website or app?
Usually, yes. Share what you have and we will advise honestly on whether to improve it or rebuild it.
What happens after the project is live?
We train your team at hand-over and stay available afterwards. An optional support plan covers fixes, small changes and data backups, so you are never left on your own.
