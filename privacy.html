Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information ZorbaTech ("we", "us") collects through zorbatech.in and how we use it. ZorbaTech is based in India.

What we collect

  • Enquiries you send us. Our contact form doesn't store anything on our website. It opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. When you message, call or email us, we receive your name, phone number or email and whatever you write.
  • Basic technical data. Like every website, our hosting providers may log standard information such as your IP address and browser type for security and performance.

We do not use advertising trackers on this website, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver and support the work you order from us.
  • To send invoices and receipts.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts to display text, and links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and our project sites. These services have their own privacy policies.

Demo apps

The demo apps on this website store any data you type only in your own browser. It is not sent to us.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your enquiry details at any time by writing to manu@zorbatech.in.
